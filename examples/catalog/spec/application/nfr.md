# Non-functional requirements

## Audit

Every proposal, approval and expiry of a purchase price or sales price records who did it and when.

## Data retention

Expired prices are kept for seven years for audits.

## Privacy and GDPR

The catalog holds no personal data apart from supplier contact persons.

## Availability

Sales staff must be able to look up prices during business hours on working days.

## Languages

English.

## Currencies

One currency (see DEC-002).
