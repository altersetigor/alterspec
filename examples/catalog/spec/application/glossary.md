# Glossary

## Article

```yaml
term: "Article"
forbidden: [item]
```

Something the company sells, identified by its article number.

## Brand

```yaml
term: "Brand"
forbidden: [trademark]
```

The name under which a manufacturer markets articles.

## Category

```yaml
term: "Category"
forbidden: [product group]
```

A group of similar articles, used to browse and to report.

## Unit of measure

```yaml
term: "Unit of measure"
forbidden: [UoM]
```

The quantity an article is bought and sold in, such as piece, box or kilogram.

## Supplier

```yaml
term: "Supplier"
forbidden: [vendor]
```

A company the organisation buys articles from.

## Purchase price

```yaml
term: "Purchase price"
forbidden: [cost price]
```

What a supplier charges for one unit of measure of an article, from a given date.

## Sales price

```yaml
term: "Sales price"
forbidden: [selling price]
```

What the company charges for one unit of measure of an article, from a given date, once approved.
