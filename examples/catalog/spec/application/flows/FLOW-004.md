# Discontinue article

## Goal

An article stops being sold everywhere at once.

## Trigger

The company stops selling an article.

## Steps

1. CAP-CAT-006 by ROLE-CATALOG-MANAGER
2. CAP-PRC-003 by ROLE-PRICING-MANAGER

## Outcome

The article is discontinued and has no valid sales price.

## Exceptions

None.
